REQUIRED
1
What were your key outputs this week?▾
List 1-3 main outputs completed this week (spec docs, prototypes, competitive analysis, flowcharts, meeting notes, etc.). For each: what you did, why, and current status.
Example: ① Completed first draft of User Center redesign spec (passed internal review) ② Delivered user flow comparison across Competitors A/B/C
2
What was the most important thing you learned this week?▾
This could be a methodology, tool, business insight, inspiration from a colleague, or a lesson from a mistake. Be specific: what you learned + how + why it matters.
Example: Learned to use User Story Mapping to prioritize features, picked up from the Product Director during a review meeting. Before, I prioritized by intuition; now I have a structured approach.
3
What was your biggest challenge this week?▾
This could be a challenged requirement, a failed communication, or a repeatedly revised proposal. Describe: what happened + how you handled it + the outcome. Be honest if unresolved — your mentor will help.
Example: My spec doc was rejected twice by engineering due to unclear acceptance criteria. I reviewed three past specs, reformatted the criteria, and it passed on the third try.
OPTIONAL
4
Where did you contribute your own perspective this week?▾
Did you speak up in meetings? What suggestions did you make? If you were mainly observing, it's fine to write "Focused on absorbing this week; did not actively speak up yet."
5
What is one product skill you most want to improve next week?▾
E.g. "Independently conduct one user interview and produce notes" or "Learn to validate a requirement with data." Break big goals into concrete, achievable actions for next week.
6
What would you like to ask your mentor?▾
Work questions, career development queries, or something you don't understand about a decision. Your mentor will respond in comments or schedule a 1-on-1.
7
Anything else you want to tell your mentor?▾
Anything you want to say but feel shy about in person: suggestions on coaching pace, or "This week felt great — thank you for your guidance."
